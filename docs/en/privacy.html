Privacy Policy

TaskFace — September 21, 2026

TaskFace does not send your reminders or events to the developer. There is no account and no server. Your data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Information we collect

None. No sign-up and no email address are needed. The app does no usage analytics and no advertising measurement.

Reminders and calendars

Within the access you allow, TaskFace reads the reminders on your iPhone and chooses what to show on the watch face. When you add, complete, rename or reorder items in TaskFace, it writes those changes to Reminders (the order is saved as priority).

If you choose to show events, TaskFace reads your calendars. It never writes to your calendars.

This information is sent from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity. Only the titles and times of the few items shown on the watch face are sent, and they never leave your Apple Watch.

Network

TaskFace never connects to the internet. There are no in-app purchases (the tip jar was removed in September 2026). Tapping “More apps by this developer” opens the App Store; it is the App Store that connects, not this app.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes the display settings and send history it kept on the device. Your reminders and calendars stay in the built-in apps.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
